What you'll learn

From idea to final cut.

Six parts, in the exact order we make every reel. Each part ends with something finished, so by the end you have a full episode ready to post.

  1. 01

    Find an idea people will share

    How we pick a world, a character and a situation that people already love, then add a twist they don't expect. You'll learn to break down a viral reel second by second and turn what you see into ten ideas of your own.

    • Claude
    • Instagram & TikTok research

    You finish with three episode ideas, ranked by how likely they are to travel.

  2. 02

    Build characters that never change face

    The master-frame method: generate a few candidates, pick one, and make every other shot as an edit of that frame. The face, hair, body and outfit stay the same in every scene. It works for realistic people, AI influencers, cartoon and game characters.

    • Higgsfield Soul
    • Nano Banana Pro

    You finish with a master frame and a character sheet you can reuse in every episode.

  3. 03

    Write the script in beats

    A hook in the first two seconds, a gag every few seconds, a twist at the end and a reason to wait for episode 2. You get the script prompts we use, plus a timing sheet that shows exactly where each beat lands.

    • Claude
    • Our script prompts

    You finish with a shot-by-shot script for your first episode.

  4. 04

    Direct the shots

    One keyframe for every beat: camera angle, lens, light and where each character stands. Then a checklist that catches the mistakes that give AI away, such as extra fingers, a wrong logo or a car with two different wheels.

    • Nano Banana Pro
    • Higgsfield

    You finish with 6 to 12 approved keyframes.

  5. 05

    Turn images into video, with voice

    Which model to use for which shot: Kling for faces and acting, Seedance for action and multi-shot scenes. You'll see the prompts that keep the spoken words intact and how to give a character its own voice. Every shot shows what it cost us in credits.

    • Kling
    • Seedance
    • Higgsfield
    • ElevenLabs

    You finish with every clip of your episode and a budget for the next one.

  6. 06

    Edit with Claude Code and post

    Claude Code edits the episode for you: music cut on the beat, sound effects, captions, a film look and the right loudness for Instagram. Then the 4:3 format, a cover that gets the tap, and the caption.

    • Claude Code
    • Remotion
    • CapCut

    You finish with a finished episode, posted.
